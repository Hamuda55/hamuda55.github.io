Terms of Service

Governs the use of "My Page Poster," a single-operator tool that schedules and publishes video to the Operator's own TikTok account(s) through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

01

What this is

My Page Poster is a personal-use tool, not a public product or service offered to third parties. It runs on infrastructure controlled by the person who deployed it (the "Operator") and acts only on TikTok account(s) the Operator owns or is otherwise authorized to manage.

02

Who may use it

Access is limited to the Operator. This tool is not distributed, resold, or made available for other people's accounts.

03

What it does

  • Publishes video the Operator supplies (hosted at a public URL) to the Operator's connected TikTok account(s), via TikTok's official post/publish/video/init endpoint.
  • Drafts captions and hashtags for a given topic using Claude (Anthropic).
  • Drafts, but never sends, candidate replies to comments the Operator manually provides — replies are queued for the Operator's own review and action.
04

What it does not do

This tool does not scrape TikTok, and does not automate likes, follows, direct messages, or comment replies. It does not attempt to read data TikTok has not exposed through an approved API scope, and it does not act on any account without a completed OAuth authorization from that account's owner.

05

Operator responsibilities

  • Comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms of Service at all times.
  • Keep API credentials and OAuth tokens confidential.
  • Disclose AI-generated video per TikTok's own policy (the tool sets is_aigc accordingly, but the Operator is responsible for accuracy).
  • Only publish content the Operator holds the rights to distribute.
06
