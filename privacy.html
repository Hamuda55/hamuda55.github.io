Privacy Policy

Describes what data "My Page Poster" handles while publishing video to the Operator's own TikTok account(s) through TikTok's official API, and drafting text through Anthropic's Claude API.

01

Scope

This tool processes data only for the Operator's own connected TikTok account(s). It is not used to collect data from the public, from viewers of the Operator's videos, or from any third party.

02

Data this tool handles

DataSourcePurpose
OAuth access & refresh tokensTikTok, after the Operator authorizes an accountAuthenticate publish/status requests
TikTok account identifierTikTok, during OAuthRoute a scheduled post to the right account
Video URL, caption, schedule timeEntered by the OperatorPublish content at the intended time
Comment textManually supplied by the OperatorDraft a candidate reply for the Operator to review
03

Where it's stored

Locally, as plain JSON files on the machine the Operator runs this tool on (data/tokens.json, data/schedule.json, data/pending_replies.json). This tool does not upload that data to any cloud service of its own.

04

Who it's shared with

  • TikTok — via its official Content Posting API, to publish the Operator's video and check publish status.
  • Anthropic — via the Claude API, to draft caption and reply text from the content/comment text the Operator supplies.

No data is sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and no analytics or tracking pixels are embedded in this tool.

05

Retention

Tokens and schedule data persist in the local files above until the Operator deletes them, or until the Operator revokes this app's access from their TikTok account settings.

06

Security

Access to these files is governed by the permissions of the machine the Operator runs this tool on. The Operator is responsible for securing that device and for not committing .env or data/tokens.json to any shared or public repository.

07

Changes

The Operator may revise this document at any time; the effective date above reflects the latest revision.
